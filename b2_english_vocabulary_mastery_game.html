Key Takeaways & Grammar Rules

instead of + [gerund / noun]: Used to express a choice made in place of another action (e.g., *instead of taking*).
suffer from [illness]: Correct preposition for diseases or medical conditions (e.g., *suffering from a cold*).
tell someone off: Phrasal verb meaning to reprimand or scold severely.
capable of + -ing: Having the ability or skill to do something.
wear off: Phrasal verb meaning to gradually diminish or disappear (e.g., novelty, pain).
tasked with + [task]: Assigned the duty or responsibility of doing something.
Prefix rules:
  • im- usually precedes root words starting with p or m (im-possible, im-patient, im-personal).
  • in- precedes words like in-effective, in-capable, in-sufficient.
  • un- is the most common general negative prefix (un-necessary, un-emotional, un-predictable).
